# Nolan portfolio working draft

This directory preserves portfolio material that is tied to the development of Aroma Wheel but is not part of the Aroma Wheel website.

## Iterations

- `nolan-authorship-portfolio-draft.html` — Iteration 01: an evidence-led authorship record with provenance filters, systems precedents, and development storyboards.
- `agent-and-human-liminal.html` — Iteration 02: a more vibrant, narrative-led telling in which a moving substrate of Nolan's prompts becomes the liminal space between lived experience, agent interpretation, correction, and shared reality.
- `REVIEW-BRIEF.md` — a provenance-aware invitation for Claude, other agents, and human reviewers to evaluate the narrative and visual hypotheses without obscuring where they came from.

Iteration 02 does not replace Iteration 01. They are preserved side by side so their different interpretations can be compared.

## Current boundary

- The draft is being developed on `codex/human-authorship-story`.
- Nothing in this directory is linked from `index.html`, `process.html`, or `story.html`.
- Do not merge or publish this directory through `prototype` until Nolan explicitly approves how the portfolio and Aroma Wheel should relate publicly.
- The standalone HTML may be opened directly for review while it remains off the deployed branch.

## Provenance

The page separates Nolan's verbatim language and documented direction from agent-written synthesis, implementation, inherited conventions, and observable artifacts. Its current structure was developed through conversation with Nolan on 2026-08-18 and remains a working draft pending his review and correction.
